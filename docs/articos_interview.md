Interview Script
pet-management
Can you tell me a bit about your pets and how you currently stay on top of their daily needs and health records?



current-tools
What kinds of tools or apps do you use now to track things like pet vaccinations, vet appointments, routines, or expenses?



document-management
Can you walk me through the last time you needed to find a specific health document or vaccination record for one of your pets?



reminders
How do you usually remember or set reminders for pet-related tasks, like giving medication or scheduling checkups?



expense-tracking
When was the most recent time you tracked or reviewed your pet-related expenses? How did you go about it?



decision-making
Thinking about the apps and tools you use for your pets, what influenced your decision to use free or paid versions?



past-experience
Have you ever tried out a premium or paid feature in a pet management or productivity app? What was that experience like?



word-of-mouth
Have you recommended any pet management tools or apps to others? If so, what made you decide to share them?



multi-pet
Can you describe a recent situation where managing multiple pets felt especially complicated or time-consuming?



satisfaction
Tell me about a time when you felt especially satisfied (or dissatisfied) with the way a tool or system helped you stay organized as a pet owner.



workflow-integration
If you had access to an all-in-one mobile app that combined document storage, routines, reminders, and expense tracking for pets, how might that fit into your current way of managing things?



Imagine an app that doesn't just store data, but uses AI to automatically analyze your pet-related expenses, predict upcoming budgets, and generate daily health summaries. How valuable would this kind of AI automation be for you compared to your current manual tracking methods?



When choosing digital tools for your daily life, how much does a premium, highly aesthetic, and clean design (like an intuitive 'bento-grid' dashboard) influence your decision to use and pay for an app, compared to a basic but functional free tool?



If a single, beautifully designed app successfully replaced your current mix of notes, alarms, and spreadsheets, and completely removed the stress of pet management with AI, what pricing model would you prefer (e.g., Freemium, Monthly Subscription, One-time fee)? And what specific monthly price point would you consider reasonable for this peace of mind?



reflection
Looking back, is there anything you wish had been easier or more streamlined about caring for your pets, either with tools you use now or in the past?
